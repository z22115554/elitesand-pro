FFmpeg (not bundled by default; downloaded on demand)
------------------------------------------------------
Elitesand Pro does not bundle ffmpeg.exe/ffprobe.exe by default. The first
time an FFmpeg-dependent feature (e.g. YouTube-to-MP3) is used, the app offers
to download the official Windows "essentials" build from gyan.dev — the
distribution gyan.dev's own build page and ffmpeg.org both point to for
Windows — and verifies its SHA-256 against a checksum fetched from the same
source before using it. The app never links FFmpeg into its own code; it is
only ever invoked as a separate process. Because Elitesand Pro itself does not
redistribute the GPLv3 binary, it carries no obligation to provide FFmpeg's
corresponding source. Users who already have FFmpeg installed, or who set
ffmpegPath in server/config.js, are not prompted to download anything.
License guidance: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
Gyan build information: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
